# The introspection report measured five feedback loops that had gone dead

**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Source:** `docs/reports/2026-09-02-introspection.md` §R4–R6 · **Cards:** T-0215, T-0220

## What happened

Forty-one days and 212 cards in, the numbers said the rules were being skipped where nothing wrote an outcome down. **Budget:** 17 cards logged overruns up to 12.5×, every one accepted ad hoc with "no budget enforcement (M2)"; the five worst were a quarter of all recorded work. **Approvals:** 83 % of blocks waited for a human yes, blocked time was 13 % of wall time with a 152 m mean, and Briefs cited registry `## Gotchas` 93 times against `## Product` 3 — the section that exists to pre-answer design questions was the one Briefs did not read. **Downstream folds:** the rule that a repo-specific lesson becomes a fold card into that project's CLAUDE.md was law for 212 cards and produced zero folds. **Close-out:** monitor's done row named a "retro-lite" no skill defined, and the improvised close-out skipped metrics and the decisions' `Outcome:` backfill — 30 of the first 197 done cards carry no metrics line; `state: failed` had no written rule and was reached by none of 212 cards. **Memory:** 12 of 36 memory files lacked a `modified:` stamp, none named its source, and karta's project memory was empty because its path had been guessed from the slug.

## What changed

The budget is a checkpoint: monitor's overrun row nudges once for a scope decision (finish within a bound, split, or hand off), logs it under a fixed label, and escalates only when no decision lands. Triage pre-answers design questions from `## Product`, citing the line, and sends what Product cannot answer to Saket in its own frontier round; every Brief tells the worker to ask in one round. Retro's learnings step ends with `downstream: T-NNNN` or `downstream: none — <reason>`, so a skipped obligation is a countable line. A verified done invokes retro whole; `failed` is monitor's verdict alone (retry ceiling, or an approach that went sideways), `abandoned` is triage's cancel. Every memory file retro writes carries `modified:` and `source: T-NNNN`; the resolved directory is recorded once on the registry card as `memory-dir:` and every writer reads it.

## Where the rule stands

Monitor's overrun and done rows; triage §4's pre-answer bullet; retro step 2's downstream gate, step 6's transition, the three-verdict paragraph and weekly step 1; `templates/registry-card.md` `memory-dir:`.
